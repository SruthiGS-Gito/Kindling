        About

        A space to get curious,
        not a test to pass.

        Kindling helps you follow what catches your attention, and see where those questions could lead.

        What Kindling is

        Kindling is a place to explore what interests you, out loud, without being scored, graded, or sorted into a "type." There's no quiz to fail here, and no single right answer to find.

        How it works

        01

        Explore

        Chat with Kindling about anything you're curious about. No script, no wrong answers.

        02

        Inference

        Kindling gently notices patterns in how you explore, never a label for who you are.

        03

        Career Graph

        See real fields and careers connected to what you've been curious about, and where they could lead.

        What Kindling isn't

        • It isn't a personality test or a career-matching quiz with a final score.
        • It doesn't decide your future, or tell you who you "should" be.
        • It isn't a replacement for a real teacher, school counselor, or someone who actually knows you.

        Where the career information comes from

        The fields, careers, and everyday tasks you see on the Career Graph come from O*NET, a free public database of U.S. occupations.

        This product uses information from O*NET Web Services by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA. Learn more at onetcenter.org.

        A note on accuracy

        Kindling's replies come from an AI, and AI can get things wrong or oversimplify. Use it to explore ideas, not as the final word. Before any big decision, like choosing a class, a major, or a path, talk it over with a real teacher, school counselor, or trusted adult.

        Privacy Policy

        Last updated:

        This policy explains what information Kindling collects and how it's used.

        What we store

        • Your account email, and a securely scrambled (hashed) version of your password, never the password itself.
        • The name you give us on sign-up, if you choose to give one - it's optional, and we fall back to the first part of your email if you leave it blank.
        • The messages you send Kindling, and Kindling's replies, so your conversation is still there when you come back.
        • Behavioral signals from how you explore, like the patterns Inference notices, which fields and careers you've opened on the Career Graph, and which patterns you've told Kindling feel right or don't.

        Why we store it

        • To keep your conversations right where you left them.
        • To notice patterns across everything you've explored, not just one chat.
        • To make the Career Graph feel personal to your exploration, instead of generic.

        Where it lives

        Everything above lives in Kindling's own database, on the server that runs the app. It isn't synced to any other storage service, aside from the AI providers below, which only ever see the text of one message at the moment they're generating a reply to it.

        Who can see it

        • You, whenever you're signed in and looking at your own exploration.
        • People on the Kindling team, for overall usage patterns and (only when needed to fix a real problem) the messages in a specific conversation. This is only ever to build and improve Kindling.

        We never sell your data, and we never share it with advertisers.

        The AI providers we use

        When you send Kindling a message, its text is sent to Groq and, if Groq is unavailable, to Google's Gemini, so one of them can write a reply. We can't independently verify what either provider does with that text beyond their own published policies, so it's best to avoid sharing anything in a chat that you wouldn't want a third-party AI service to process.

        Downloading or deleting your data

        There's no one-click "download everything" button yet. You can permanently delete your conversations, patterns, and Career Graph at any time from Settings → Reset exploration, which really deletes it, not just hides it. Deleting your account entirely isn't self-serve yet either; email us at hello@kindling.app and we'll take care of it by hand.

        If you're under 13

        Please explore Kindling together with a parent or guardian.

        Terms of Use

        Last updated:

        These terms explain how Kindling can be used and what it can and can't do for you.

        Using Kindling

        • Kindling is meant for personal, non-commercial exploring, not for automated use, scraping, or reselling what it shows you.
        • Be a decent person here: don't try to break, overload, or misuse Kindling, and don't use it to generate anything harmful.

        About what Kindling tells you

        Kindling's replies, patterns, and career suggestions are generated by AI and by general career data (from O*NET); they're starting points for exploring, not professional, medical, legal, or financial advice.

        AI can be wrong, outdated, or oversimplified. Always check anything important with a real teacher, school counselor, or trusted adult, especially before a decision that matters.

        Your account

        Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for what happens in your account. You can reset your exploration or ask us to delete your account at any time. See the Privacy Policy for how.

        Changes

        Kindling is still actively being built, so these terms, and the app itself, may change over time. We'll try to keep this page current.
